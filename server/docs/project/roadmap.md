# PenniWise — Product Roadmap

## 1. Introduction

This roadmap defines the planned progression of PenniWise from product development and regulatory preparation through private beta, public launch, growth, and deeper product expansion.

The roadmap is based on the current PenniWise product brief and is intended to serve as a high-level guide for product, engineering, partnerships, operations, and growth activities.

---

## 2. Roadmap Overview

The current roadmap is organized into five major stages:

1. Pre-seed and infrastructure preparation
2. Private beta
3. Public launch
4. Growth phase
5. Product depth and Series A preparation

The roadmap described in the product brief covers Q3 2025 through Q4 2026.

---

# 3. Phase 1 — Pre-Seed and Foundation

## Timeline

**Q3 2025**

## Objectives

The primary objective of this phase is to secure the resources, partnerships, and team required to move PenniWise from concept toward a working product.

## Key Activities

### Fundraising

Complete the pre-seed fundraising round.

The investor brief identifies a target raise of:

**N150M**

with:

* 10% equity offered
* N1.5B pre-money valuation

### Regulatory Partnerships

Begin and progress partnership discussions with:

* Brokerage partners
* MFB/deposit-holding partners
* KYC providers
* Other required infrastructure providers

The broker and MFB partnership model is central to the intended PenniWise operating structure.

### Team Building

Hire:

* CTO
* Two engineers

The goal is to establish the core technical team responsible for product development and infrastructure.

### Product and Technical Foundation

Build the foundations required for:

* WhatsApp Business API integration
* User onboarding
* KYC
* Savings
* Trading
* Wallet infrastructure
* AI interaction layer
* Backend services
* Database infrastructure
* External financial integrations

---

# 4. Phase 2 — Private Beta

## Timeline

**Q4 2025**

## Objectives

Launch a controlled beta version of PenniWise and validate the core user experience before public launch.

## Key Activities

### WhatsApp Business API

Make the WhatsApp Business API experience operational.

### End-to-End KYC

Complete the full onboarding and KYC process, including:

* BVN
* NIN
* Liveness
* Risk profiling

### Beta Users

Launch with approximately:

**500 beta users**

These users should provide real-world feedback on onboarding, conversation quality, KYC, savings, and other early product flows.

### Key Validation Areas

The private beta should validate:

* Ease of onboarding
* Conversation quality
* KYC completion
* User understanding of financial actions
* Reliability of WhatsApp interactions
* Savings experience
* Trading experience where enabled
* Human escalation process

---

# 5. Phase 3 — Public Launch

## Timeline

**Q1 2026**

## Objectives

Move from controlled beta testing to a public-facing product.

## Key Activities

### Full Trading

Launch NGX trading capabilities.

Users should be able to buy and sell supported NGX-listed securities through the conversational experience.

### Savings Wallet

Launch the savings wallet with the intended flexible and locked savings experiences.

### Marketing Activation

Begin focused marketing activity in:

* Lagos
* Abuja

### User Target

Target:

**5,000 users**

by the public-launch stage.

---

# 6. Phase 4 — Growth Phase

## Timeline

**Q2 2026**

## Objectives

Increase user acquisition, strengthen retention, and introduce distribution partnerships.

## Key Activities

### Referral Programme

Launch a referral programme designed to encourage existing users to introduce new customers to PenniWise.

### Payroll Savings Partnerships

Develop payroll savings partnerships with approximately:

**3 employers**

This is intended to create a repeatable savings acquisition channel.

### User Target

Target:

**20,000 users** during the growth phase.

---

# 7. Phase 5 — Product Depth

## Timeline

**Q3–Q4 2026**

## Objectives

Expand the intelligence and breadth of the PenniWise product.

## Key Activities

### AI Investment Recommendations

Introduce deeper AI-powered investment recommendations based on:

* Risk profile
* Portfolio composition
* Market information

Recommendations should remain clearly identified as suggestions rather than financial advice.

### Fixed-Income Products

Expand beyond equities into fixed-income investment opportunities.

The investor brief specifically mentions products such as:

* T-bills
* Bonds
* Mutual funds

through relevant partner institutions.

### Pidgin English NLP

Introduce Nigerian Pidgin English support to make conversational financial interaction more accessible to a broader audience.

The broader language roadmap also identifies future support for:

* Yoruba
* Hausa
* Igbo.

### Series A Preparation

Use the expanded product, user base, and operational data to prepare for a potential Series A fundraising process.

# 9. Partnership Roadmap

PenniWise's product development is dependent on external financial and infrastructure partnerships.

## Brokerage

The brokerage partner is expected to provide:

* NGX access
* CSCS account opening
* Order execution
* Trading infrastructure

Potential partners identified in the investor brief include Morgan Capital Securities and Citi Investment Securities.

## MFB

The MFB partner is expected to provide:

* Deposit holding
* Savings wallet infrastructure
* NIP transfers
* Banking infrastructure
* Applicable AML/CFT processes

Potential partners identified include Crest MFB and Safe Haven MFB.

## KYC

The product brief identifies KYC providers such as:

* Smile Identity
* Dojah

for identity and liveness verification.

# 11. Revenue Roadmap

The business model is expected to progressively develop multiple revenue streams.

### Brokerage

Revenue from commissions on NGX trades.

### Savings

Revenue from the spread between the MFB deposit rate and the rate passed to customers.

### Premium

Subscription revenue from advanced features.

### Fixed Income

Referral revenue from partner AMCs and financial institutions.

### Institutional Data

Potential revenue from anonymized and aggregated retail sentiment data.

The investor brief projects:

* Year 1: N180M
* Year 2: N620M
* Year 3: N1.8B

based on the stated active-user assumptions.

# 13. Roadmap Management

This roadmap is a living document.

As PenniWise develops, planned features may:

* Move between phases.
* Be added.
* Be removed.
* Be delayed.
* Be replaced.
* Be reprioritized based on user feedback, regulatory requirements, technical dependencies, and business needs.

Changes to the roadmap should be discussed and agreed upon by the project team before being reflected here.

Major roadmap changes should also be reflected in the relevant:

* Requirements documentation
* Architecture documentation
* Database documentation
* API documentation
* AI documentation
* WhatsApp documentation
* Banking documentation
* Brokerage documentation
* Changelog
